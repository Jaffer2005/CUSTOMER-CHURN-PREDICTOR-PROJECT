Model Documentation

Understanding the RetainIQ Churn Prediction Engine

🧠 Architecture

The predictor is powered by an XGBoost Gradient Boosting Classifier, trained on over 300,000 historical customer retention records spanning 5 years across diverse geographical regions.

Framework:XGBoost v2.0 Accuracy:92.4% (F1: 0.89) Input Features:16 Variables Output:Binary Probability
🛡️ Interpreting Risk

The model produces a raw probability score between 0.0 and 1.0. We interpret this score into actionable tiers:

Low Risk (Stay) < 30%
High probability of renewal.
Medium Risk (Warning) 30% – 59%
Showing early signs of disengagement.
High Risk (Churn) ≥ 60%
Immediate intervention recommended.
🗄️ Feature Importance Dictionary
How the model weights different inputs
High Impact Primary Drivers
  • Contract Type: Monthly contracts carry significantly higher risk than Annual.
  • Tenure Months: Risk decreases exponentially after month 14.
  • Satisfaction Score: Strong negative correlation with churn.
  • Monthly Charges: Sudden spikes or high MRR relative to usage increases risk.
Medium Impact Secondary Drivers
  • Support Calls: >3 calls in 6 months indicates friction.
  • Payment Delay Days: Consistent late payments correlate with financial churn.
  • Usage Hours: Drop in week-over-week usage is a strong leading indicator.
  • Number of Services: Multi-product customers (sticky) churn less.
Low Impact Demographic & Tertiary
  • Age & Gender: Minor demographic correlations.
  • Region: Used mostly for macro-trend normalization.
  • Payment Method: Auto-debit methods perform slightly better than manual payment.
